## PART 2: THE REAL PROBLEMS (Ranked by What Hotels Will PAY FOR)

### **#1 PROBLEM: DIRTY ROOM REVIEWS = LOST REVENUE**

#### **The Reality:**
- Top reasons for 1-star reviews on TripAdvisor/Booking.com:
  1. "Room was dirty" (35% of bad reviews)
  2. "Staff didn't respond to requests" (25%)
  3. "Poor check-in experience" (15%)

#### **How It Happens:**
```
Guest checks out Room 201 at 11 AM
↓
Supervisor is busy, doesn't check quality
↓
Housekeeper marks it clean (even if questionable)
↓
New guest arrives 3 PM, finds:
  - Stain on carpet
  - Hair in bathroom
  - Old towels not replaced
  - Dust on furniture
↓
Guest posts 1-star review: "Dirty room, wouldn't come back"
↓
This review tanks the hotel's rating
↓
Next 100 booking prospects see 4.2-star hotel instead of 4.8-star
↓
REVENUE LOST: $500K-1M annually
```

#### **Why Current System Fails:**
- Supervisor can't physically inspect 100+ rooms before next check-in
- "Looks clean" subjective (supervisor sees dirty room as "acceptable")
- No photo documentation (he-said-she-said with housekeeper)
- Quality issues only caught when guest complains (too late)

#### **What Hotels Will Pay For:**
"**AI that catches every dirty room BEFORE guest arrives**"

- Hotel manager: "If you can prevent 'dirty room' complaints, you're worth $200K/year"
- Why? Because one 1-star review = $5-10K in lost revenue
- Preventing 20 dirty room reviews = $100-200K in saved revenue

## PART 4: THE REAL PROBLEM TO SOLVE

### **If You Had to Pick ONE Problem to Solve:**

**DIRTY ROOM PREVENTION**

**Why:**
- Highest revenue impact ($500K-1M prevented)
- Hotels will pay $200K+/year to solve this
- Simplest to implement (photo verification system)
- Strongest emotional hook ("Never let a guest see a dirty room again")
- Biggest competitive advantage (nobody is doing this yet)

**How It Works:**
```
Housekeeper finishes Room 301
↓
Takes 3 photos (bed, bathroom, general room)
↓
AI instantly reviews photos against checklist:
  ✓ Bed clean? (sheets tucked, no stains)
  ✓ Bathroom spotless? (toilet clean, mirrors clean, no hair)
  ✓ Floors clean? (vacuumed, corners dust-free, no debris)
  ✓ Furniture dusted? (no stains, no visible dirt)
  ✓ Supplies stocked? (toiletries, coffee, notepads)
↓
AI says "PASS" or "FAIL - redo bathroom, stain on carpet"
↓
If PASS: Room marked ready, guest can check in
If FAIL: Housekeeper fixes, retakes photos, passes on 2nd try
↓
RESULT: 100% of rooms pass quality check before guest arrival
```

**Why Hotels Will Buy This:**
- Prevents the #1 complaint: "dirty room"
- Prevents the #1 threat to ratings
- Prevents revenue loss from low ratings
- Data shows which housekeepers need training
- Hotels see ROI immediately (fewer complaints = higher ratings = more bookings)

## PART 7: THE HONEST TRUTH ABOUT TRELLIS

**What Trellis is selling:** "AI agents for vacation rental operations"
**What they're REALLY selling:** "Better guest service = higher ratings = more bookings"

**What makes Trellis work:**
1. Vacation rental market is desperate for automation (fragmented systems)
2. Guests expect instant responses (Airbnb trained them)
3. Any improvement in responsiveness = better ratings = higher occupancy

**Why this is different for hotels:**
1. Hotels have consolidated PMS already (no system fragmentation pain)
2. Guests expect staff, not automation (different market expectations)
3. Hotels' biggest pain is OPERATIONAL QUALITY, not responsiveness

**So the real solution for hotels is:**
- Quality assurance system (AI-powered inspection)
- Guest service (fills the gap when staff can't)
- Revenue capture (upsells)

NOT just "agents on top of fragmented systems" (Trellis model).
