## PART 4: HOSPEDIA vs TRELLIS — STRATEGIC TRADE-OFFS

### **IF YOU BUILD HOSPEDIA (Your Current Path)**

**✅ Advantages:**
1. **Higher revenue per customer** — Hotel operations are complex; they'll pay $200-500K for integrated system
2. **Stronger switching costs** — Hotels embed your system into daily ops across HR, Finance, Ops, Sales
3. **Larger TAM** — Hotels are bigger than vacation rentals (even though VR is growing faster)
4. **Competitive moat** — Full ecosystem is harder to replicate than agent layer
5. **Venture-friendly** — SaaS investors love enterprise software with high ACV

**❌ Challenges:**
1. **Longer sales cycle** — Hotels make decisions slowly (3-6 months typical)
2. **Higher implementation cost** — You need to integrate with their existing systems, migrate data, train staff
3. **More competition** — Hotel PMS market is mature (Opera, Horus, Elinext, etc.)
4. **Regulatory/compliance** — Hotels have labor laws, PCI compliance, tax reporting requirements per jurisdiction
5. **Higher execution risk** — Building 8-10 modules takes more engineering than 1 agent
6. **Longer time to revenue** — You need more features before first customer pays

**Revenue/Customer Model:**
- ACV: $200-500K (estimated)
- Sales cycle: 3-6 months
- Implementation: 2-3 months
- Time to first customer: 6-9 months
- Customer lifetime value: $1M+ (3-5 year relationship)

---

### **IF YOU BECOME "HOSPEDIA AGENTS" (Pivot to Trellis Model)**

**✅ Advantages:**
1. **Faster time to market** — Build 1-2 agents, ship in 3 months vs 9+ months
2. **Lower implementation burden** — You plug into their existing PMS (like Trellis does)
3. **Faster sales cycles** — Property managers see value in 2-4 weeks
4. **Proven model** — Trellis is showing it works ($500K ARR in 2 months)
5. **Capital efficient** — Less engineering needed; focus on AI quality instead
6. **Easier geographic expansion** — Agents work across languages/regions with less customization

**❌ Challenges:**
1. **Lower revenue per customer** — Agents are narrower; may only capture $50-150K per customer
2. **Weaker switching costs** — Hotels can easily disable your agents and use alternative tools
3. **Higher churn risk** — Without ecosystem lock-in, customers can leave when better competitor arrives
4. **Commodity risk** — Other companies can build agents faster (Open AI, Anthropic, Google can do this)
5. **Harder to defend** — Your IP is in the agents, not the system; easier for others to copy
6. **Lower valuation** — VCs typically value agent-layer companies at 2-3x revenue (vs 8-12x for platform companies)

**Revenue/Customer Model:**
- ACV: $50-150K (estimated)
- Sales cycle: 1-3 months
- Implementation: 2-4 weeks
- Time to first customer: 3-4 months
- Customer lifetime value: $200-400K (higher churn)
